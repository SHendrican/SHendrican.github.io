Product areas I work in

BDI Canada line card

Bearings

Ball & roller bearings, mounted units, precision machine-tool bearings, linear guides and shafting.

Typical applications: mounted units for conveyors, precision bearings for machine tools, linear guides for automation.

Power transmission

Couplings (incl. CENTAFLEX), belts, chain & sprockets, gearboxes and drive components.

Typical applications: couplings and drives for pumps, conveyors, mixers and packaging lines.

Hydraulics & pneumatics

Bosch Rexroth hydraulics and pneumatics, hose and fittings, pumps and valves.

Typical applications: cylinders, pumps and valves for presses, material handling and plant equipment.

Seals & fluid sealing

Rotary and hydraulic seals, O-rings, seal kits and aftermarket equivalents by equipment model.

Typical applications: seal kits for hydraulic cylinders and gearboxes, sealing for washdown and process equipment.

Safety & identification

Brady lockout/tagout, labels, tags and signage for plant safety and compliance.

Typical applications: lockout/tagout for maintenance, labels for panels, pipes and hazardous areas.

Fittings & fluid power

Tube and pipe fittings (Tubefit and others), fluid connectors and related components.

Typical applications: tube and hose connections for hydraulic, pneumatic and process lines.

How I learn a product

My working method

Product→Application→Customer problem

Every new product line starts with the manufacturer’s own training, then how the part is actually applied in the plant, then the failure or problem the customer is trying to solve. I keep a running problem library from real orders — what failed, what crossed to what, and which supplier route worked — so the next quote starts from experience, not a catalogue search.
